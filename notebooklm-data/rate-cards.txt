## PRICING NOTES

### Volume Discounts
- 3+ months engagement: 5% discount
- 6+ months engagement: 10% discount
- 12+ months engagement: 15% discount

### Team Composition Recommendations

**Small Project (< 3 months):**
- 1 Senior Developer
- 2 Mid-Level Developers
- 1 QA Engineer
- Estimated Monthly Cost: $46,000 - $52,000

**Medium Project (3-6 months):**
- 1 Tech Lead
- 2 Senior Developers
- 3 Mid-Level Developers
- 1 UI/UX Designer
- 1 DevOps Engineer
- 1 QA Engineer
- 1 Project Manager
- Estimated Monthly Cost: $140,000 - $155,000

**Large Project (6+ months):**
- 1 Solution Architect
- 2 Tech Leads
- 4 Senior Developers
- 4 Mid-Level Developers
- 2 UI/UX Designers
- 2 DevOps Engineers
- 2 QA Engineers
- 1 Project Manager
- Estimated Monthly Cost: $270,000 - $295,000

### Additional Notes
- All rates include overhead and benefits
- Rates may vary based on specific expertise required
- Onshore rates may be 20-30% higher than offshore
- Part-time engagements available at hourly rates
- Dedicated teams available with long-term commitments
- All team members are fluent in English
- Overlap with client time zones can be arranged
